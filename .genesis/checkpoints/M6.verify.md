### T5 — Concurrency note (non-blocking)
- `_check_redis` uses a **pipeline** (batch) but not a single atomic Lua script; under heavy parallel load, count could theoretically overshoot `max_requests` by a small margin before `zrem` rollback on the losing request. The multi-worker test asserts **exact** `total_allowed == max_requests`, which is stricter than production needs but may occasionally flake on CI. **Follow-up candidate:** Lua `EVAL` for check-and-add. Does not block M6 scope (global enforcement vs per-worker memory is the milestone win).
